Key Notes

Textbook concepts from OpenStax Psychology 2e (paraphrased).

16.1 Past and Present §16.1, ≈pp. 598–601

  • From supernatural treatments/asylums to Pinel & Dix reforms; deinstitutionalization; today’s short-stay hospitals + outpatient care by diverse professionals.

16.2 Types of Treatment §16.2, ≈pp. 602–615

  • Psychotherapy vs biomedical therapy.
  • Psychoanalysis/psychodynamic (free association, dream analysis, transference); play therapy; behavior therapy (counterconditioning, exposure, systematic desensitization, aversive conditioning, token economies).
  • Cognitive & CBT; humanistic (Rogers client-centered: active listening, UPR, genuineness, empathy).
  • Biomedical: psychotropic meds classes as surveyed; ECT; newer brain-stimulation themes as in the chapter.

中文補充：行為治療改「行為」，認知／CBT 改「想法＋行為」，人本重「關係條件」。

16.3 Treatment Modalities §16.3, ≈pp. 616–619

  • Individual, group, couples, family therapy formats; intake / confidentiality / legal limits themes.

16.4 Substance-Related Disorders §16.4, ≈pp. 620–621

  • Comorbidities; group formats (e.g., 12-step); sociocultural context of addiction treatment.

16.5 Sociocultural Model & Utilization §16.5, ≈pp. 622–632

  • Cultural competence; barriers to treatment (stigma, access, insurance, ethnicity/language); involuntary treatment issues as discussed.

Further Theoretical Depth

External journals and classic books beyond the textbook sketch.

Wolpe’s systematic desensitization is the classic counterconditioning package for phobias.

Wolpe, J. (1958). Psychotherapy by reciprocal inhibition. Stanford University Press. Google Books. Related: Wolpe, J. (1961). The systematic desensitization treatment of neuroses. Journal of Nervous and Mental Disease, 132, 189–203. doi.org/10.1097/00005053-196103000-00001

Beck’s CBT manualized approach targets automatic thoughts, cognitive distortions, and behavioral experiments.

Beck, A. T. (1979). Cognitive therapy of depression. Guilford Press. archive.org

Rogers’s necessary-and-sufficient conditions paper is the humanistic therapy cornerstone.

Rogers, C. R. (1957). The necessary and sufficient conditions of therapeutic personality change. Journal of Consulting Psychology, 21(2), 95–103. doi.org/10.1037/h0045357

Smith and Glass’s early meta-analysis helped establish psychotherapy’s average effectiveness against controls.

Smith, M. L., & Glass, G. V. (1977). Meta-analysis of psychotherapy outcome studies. American Psychologist, 32(9), 752–760. doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.32.9.752
